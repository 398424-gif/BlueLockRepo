# Panel of Experts

Have a discussion with AI about your idea for your Fan Project. Your goal is to figure out what type of professionals you need on your “Panel of Experts”. Every project must be guided by a panel of at least 4 experts. 

Once you have your Panel of Experts, you will create a persistent space inside of your AI chatbot (Project or Gem). This is where you will save the definitions / descriptions of your Panel of Experts and the concept for your fan project. 

Then you will share the MVP requirements below, and you will start to design your project with the help of your Panel of Experts. 

---

# MVP Requirements

**Clear Purpose & Target Audience**  
Identify the fandom, target user, and at least one meaningful problem the app solves or purpose it serves for that community.

**Database & Persistent Data**  
All applications must connect to a database (e.g., PostgreSQL, MongoDB, SQLite, Supabase, Firebase). All user-created, updated, or deleted data must persist across sessions and page refreshes.

**User Accounts & Authentication**  
Applications must support user accounts, including registration, login, logout, and protected routes or actions (e.g., only logged-in users can create or edit content).

**At Least 5 Unique Domain Views**  
The application must include **at least 5 distinct, functional views or screens** that directly serve the app's fan-focused topic.  
**Excluded from count:** Authentication and administrative views—such as Login, Sign Up, Password Reset, Account Settings, and Profile Management—**do not count** toward this 5-view requirement.  
*Example 5-view layout for a Basketball Fan App:*

* *View 1:* Home / Dashboard (Trending stats, featured news, quick links)  
* *View 2:* Player Directory & Search (Browse all players with filtering)  
* *View 3:* Player Detail View (Deep dive stats, career history)  
* *View 4:* Head-to-Head Comparison View (Compare two players side-by-side)  
* *View 5:* Roster / Lineup Builder (Build and save custom fantasy lineups)

**Navigation**  
Functional, intuitive navigation (e.g., navbar, sidebar, bottom bar) connecting all views with a consistent design layout.

**Full CRUD Functionality**  
Users must be able to Create, Read, Update, and Delete at least one primary domain model/entity (e.g., custom teams, song rankings, review posts, player watchlists).

**Meaningful Seed Content**  
At least 8 relevant entries containing rich, domain-specific information (not generic "Lorem Ipsum" or placeholder test data).

**Search & Filtering**  
Users can search, sort, or filter data across the primary collection views.

**Fan-Specific Interactive Feature**  
At least one advanced domain interaction beyond standard CRUD operations (e.g., interactive trivia quiz, comparison tool, tier-list generator, matchup simulator, or ranking algorithm).

**Responsive Design**  
Fully functional and visual parity across mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints.

**Validation & Feedback**  
Client/server input validation, user feedback messages (success toasts, error banners), and graceful handling of empty states.

**Deployment**  
The live application must be accessible via a publicly reachable URL (e.g., Vercel, Render, Netlify, Railway, Fly.io).

# 10 Custom Features

1. Search Bar: Search website content by keyword.  
2. Category Filters: Filter content by topic, type, or category.  
3. Sorting Options: Sort content alphabetically, by date, or popularity.  
4. Favorites Button: Save favorite items for later viewing.  
5. Rating System: Rate items using stars or a numerical scale.  
6. Image Gallery: Display collections of images.  
7. Image Carousel: Navigate through multiple featured images.  
8. Embedded Video Player: Display relevant YouTube or other videos.  
9. Dark/Light Mode: Toggle between visual themes.  
10. Countdown Timer: Display time until an upcoming event or release.  
11. Random Item Generator: Display a random item from the database.  
12. Top 10 List: Display the highest-ranked or most popular items.  
13. Poll or Voting Widget: Let visitors vote on a topic.  
14. Progress Bar: Visualize completion toward a collection or goal.  
15. Tag System: Categorize content using descriptive tags.  
16. Related Items: Suggest similar content based on categories or tags.  
17. Share Button: Copy a link or share content with others.  
18. Spoiler Toggle: Hide information until the user chooses to reveal it.  
19. Interactive Timeline: Display important events in chronological order.  
20. Comparison Tool: Compare two items side by side.  
21. Trivia Question: Ask a multiple-choice question and display the result.  
22. Recently Viewed: Show items the visitor previously opened.  
23. Personal Notes: Allow users to save notes about favorite items.  
24. Expandable Information Sections: Show or hide additional details using accordions.  
25. Back to Top Button: Return visitors to the top of a long page.  
26. User Profile Card: Display a user's name, avatar, and favorite interests.  
27. Comment Section: Allow visitors to leave comments on content.  
28. Like/Reaction Button: React to content with likes or emojis.  
29. Bookmark Collection: Organize saved items into custom lists.  
30. Recently Added: Highlight the newest content on the website.  
31. Trending Section: Showcase the most viewed, liked, or rated items.  
32. Featured Item: Highlight a randomly selected or specially chosen item.  
33. Daily Fun Fact: Display an interesting fact related to the fandom.  
34. Quote Generator: Show memorable quotes related to the topic.  
35. Fun Fact Submission Form: Allow users to contribute interesting facts.  
36. Achievement Badges: Award badges for completing activities or milestones.  
37. Activity History: Display a user's recent actions on the website.  
38. Personal Ranking List: Allow users to rank their favorite items.  
39. Wish List: Let users track items they want to experience or collect.  
40. Collection Checklist: Mark items as collected, watched, read, or completed.  
41. Calendar Widget: Display upcoming events, releases, or important dates.  
42. Notification Center: Display updates, reminders, or activity alerts.  
43. Customizable Dashboard: Allow users to rearrange or select dashboard widgets.  
44. Interactive FAQ: Provide answers to frequently asked questions.  
45. Contact/Feedback Form: Let visitors submit suggestions or report problems.  
46. User Statistics Card: Display counts of favorites, ratings, or completed activities.  
47. Content Submission Form: Allow users to recommend new items for the website.  
48. Multi-Select Filter: Filter results using several categories simultaneously.  
49. Pagination Controls: Navigate large collections across multiple pages.  
50. Download/Export Button: Export a personal list or collection as a CSV file.
